Frequently Asked Questions

What types of tires and vehicles does Gray Commercial Tire and Auto service?

We work on commercial fleet trucks, work vans, heavy-duty pickups, SUVs, and passenger cars. Our shop handles commercial and passenger tires and wheels along with precision balancing and rotation.

Do you handle mechanical auto repair in addition to tires?

Yes. Alongside tires and wheels, we provide oil changes, brake service, front end repair, vehicle diagnostics, wheel alignments, suspension work, and general mechanical repair at our shop in Gray, GA.

Do you install lift kits, leveling kits, and aftermarket bumpers?

Yes. We install lift and leveling kits and service or install bumpers, and we can perform wheel alignments and tire balancing in-house.

Does Gray Commercial Tire and Auto offer roadside service?

Yes. We offer roadside service when your vehicle needs help away from the shop. Call (478) 936-0014 to speak with our team about your location and vehicle.

Where is Gray Commercial Tire and Auto located and what areas do you serve?

Our shop is located at 121 Homer Roberts Rd, Gray, GA 31032. We serve drivers and commercial fleets across Gray, Jones County, Macon, and Milledgeville.

What are your shop hours?

We are open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET, and closed on Saturday and Sunday.

How do I schedule service or request a quote?

Call us directly at (478) 936-0014 Monday through Friday between 8 AM and 6 PM, or submit the online quote request form on this page with your name, phone number, vehicle details, and needed service.
